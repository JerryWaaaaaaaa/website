### Integration notes for Curry
- **No new dependencies.** Relies only on existing `react-router-dom` `useSearchParams`/`Link` and existing CSS tokens — nothing to install.
- **`fillerPosts()` is prototype-only** — drop it entirely and source real posts from your CMS/API. Real posts must carry a `category` in `BLOG_CATEGORIES` and (optionally) a `product` matching `ProductKey` for filtering to work.
- **Browser-support gotcha:** the empty-state entry animation uses `@starting-style` and `color-mix()` (Baseline-newer). Graceful in unsupported browsers (no animation, solid fallback needed for `color-mix`), but confirm against production's browser matrix.
- **Filter/URL contract:** filters are URL-state driven — multiple `?tag=` params + optional `?product=`; changing any filter always resets to page 1 (drops `?page=`). Invalid tag/product params are silently ignored (fall back to "All"). Preserve this if you swap in a real router.
