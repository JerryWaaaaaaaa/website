### Integration notes for Curry
- DESIGN.md is now the **only** design artifact — don't look for or recreate a `design-system/` folder; it's gone. Component CSS lives solely in `src/components/*.tsx` + `src/index.css`; DESIGN.md deliberately doesn't duplicate it.

- `src/lib/designDoc.ts`'s `sectionHtml()` matches on exact heading text (case-insensitive) — if you rename a DESIGN.md heading, update the corresponding `sectionHtml('...')` call in the matching styleguide page or it'll silently render empty.
- The YAML front-matter in DESIGN.md is the token manifest of record — when porting to production tooling (Style Dictionary, Theo, etc.), that front-matter block is the structured data to parse rather than scraping the prose tables.
