### Integration notes for Curry
- **Dependencies:** no new npm packages are visible in the diff. The only external addition is Geist Mono 500 from Google Fonts in `index.html`. Self-host or bundle it if production CSP or performance requires.
- **Assets:** everything in `public/v6/` is exported from Figma, cropped and converted to webp.
  - The Paper, Canvas and Data table slideshow backgrounds are hue-shifted from the Figma originals, so they won't match the Figma file exactly.
  - The SVGs carry Figma export cruft (`preserveAspectRatio="none"`, `overflow="visible"`, Figma ids) and should be cleaned up or swapped for the design-system icon set.
  - Many backgrounds are about 0.6–1 MB each (hero, CTA, slideshow, story). Expect to re-compress and add responsive sizes and lazy loading.
- **Tokens:** `DESIGN.md` is the source of truth. The V6 tokens are additive, so don't touch the existing ones. Rose and the text gradient are V6-only.
- **Prototype-only:** the version toggle and `pageVersions` chrome-override mechanism, and `tsconfig.app.tsbuildinfo`. In production, V6 is the page, so the toggle and V1–V5 can be dropped.
